How it works

A grid of squares of side s lies over the shape. Each square is classified by how much of it the shape covers.

  • Amin: squares entirely inside the shape.
  • Aborder: squares crossed by the border.
  • Amax = Amin + Aborder: squares needed to contain the shape.
  • Ā = (Amin + Amax)/2 and δ = (Amax − Amin)/2 (semi-dispersion). Result: Ā ± δ.
  • Aest: inside squares plus border squares covered at least as much as the threshold (50% by default).

Shapes

Pick a shape, roll a random one, or draw your own. Draw replaces the shape, Part adds a separate piece (outside the shape or inside a hole), Hole cuts a hole (draw it inside the shape). A border can never cross itself or another border: strokes that would cross are refused.

Manual mode

The view zooms to each border square in turn and you decide whether it counts. Borderline squares (shape covering less than 5% or more than 95%) also ask whether they belong to Amin or Amax at all. Keys: Y/Enter count, N skip, I inside, O outside, ← back. When you finish, crosses mark the squares where you disagree with the automatic count.

Exact area

The app knows the exact area of the shape (computed from its outline). It stays hidden so you can measure without knowing the answer. The eye button reveals it in purple: as Atrue in the table, as a purple line on the number line and on the series plots. Comparing it with the mean of many measurements shows whether a counting rule has a systematic error.

Series

Repeat the measurement with the shape in new positions. The histogram compares the spread of the results with a Gaussian of the same mean and standard deviation.
